Student Feedback

Qualitative feedback from the 2026 student module evaluations (Section C).

Student evaluation · IURI271Qualitative feedback

IURI271: what students liked most

Students highlighted the practical engagement, the video assignments, the lecturer’s approachability and the study material. Suggestions included more practice questions and a prescribed textbook.

  • The video assignments were fun and challenged my creativity. Ms Mokgoko also engaged alot with us in the class which made the lectures engaging and not boring.
  • The engagement with the lecturer. She made it easier to understand and grasp onto the concepts quicker and effectively.
  • The lecturer made students very comfortable to approach her with questions across multiple platforms.
  • This module was the most fun I've had at the NWU.

Student evaluation · PPLM824Qualitative feedback

PPLM824: what students liked most

Students valued the guest speakers and symposium, and how the module linked international law to real-world issues. Suggestions included extending the module and adding more peer-to-peer activities.

  • She brought industry role-players to come and discuss the practical side of the module with us. We learned a lot from those symposium. Very important.
  • I appreciated how the module connected international legal principles to real-world issues and current global challenges.
  • I do think that the symposium must continue. They are extremely educational and important.
  • I am happy with the structure and way of presentation by the Lecturer.
